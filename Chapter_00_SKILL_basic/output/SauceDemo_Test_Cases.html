Open Questions / Assumptions

  • Assumed product inventory comprises six products. Exact product names, descriptions, images, sort order, prices, and any variation among known-defect accounts should be confirmed against the live build.
  • Expected cart persistence after a browser refresh and after logout/login is taken from the requested coverage; confirm the intended persistence contract because this is a demo application.
  • Checkout validation messages, maximum field lengths, accepted characters, tax rate/rounding, payment/shipping display values, and exact confirmation copy are not specified. Assert required-field blocking and arithmetic consistency; confirm exact copy and policy with the product owner.
